A QUICK READING GUIDE

The notes are your map.

The upper staff uses treble clef; the lower uses bass clef. Each has five lines. A step to the next line or space moves to the next letter name.

Treble: E4 is the bottom line. Middle C is on a short ledger line below. Bass: C3 is the second space from the bottom. Middle C is on a ledger line above.

Filled note + stem = 1 beat.
Hollow note + stem = 2 beats.
Hollow note without a stem = 4 beats.
A flag = ½ beat. A dot adds half the original value.

Small numbers above notes are fingers. The highlighted column follows the example, then waits for your correct note in practice.

This is an unbarred learning score, with one note at a time. Pitch feedback does not assess rhythm, fingering, or simultaneous chords.
